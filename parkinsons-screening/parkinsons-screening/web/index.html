Screening support, not diagnosis. Nothing here is a medical diagnosis. Talk to a qualified clinician about any health concern.

Parkinson's screening support

Two independent modules. They are deliberately not combined: voice and drawing data come from different people, so a fused score would be fabricated. Audio and images are processed in memory and never stored.

Checking server…

1 · Voice module

A. Paste a UCI-format feature row real model

22 pre-computed UCI features (optionally preceded by a name and containing the UCI status column, which is ignored).

B. Record or upload a sustained vowel unvalidated demo

Features are approximated in your browser and are not computed like the UCI data. Any score is an unvalidated demo, not comparable to the training data.

2 · Spiral-drawing module

Upload a photo/scan of a spiral on paper, or draw one below. A drawing made with a mouse or finger on screen differs from the paper/tablet data used for training, so treat on-screen drawings as a demo only.

Limitations: small datasets, subject-wise performance with wide uncertainty, no external validation. See the README.
